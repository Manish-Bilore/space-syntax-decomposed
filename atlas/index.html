Map atlas

Generated by scripts/12_atlas.py from the Phase 0–1 outputs. Maps show interior streets; classes are quintiles within each panel (ranks, not raw values, are comparable between panels and cities). Divergence maps show the percentile-rank difference between two measures: thin grey where they agree within 10 points. Street data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Study network: Copenhagen + Frederiksberg (cleaned 10 m)

Interior streets (blue) are analysed and reported; buffer streets (grey) only give them a full catchment. 30,051 interior streets, 2,232 km, 21.6 km per km². Red squares: the 3 × 3 km zoom windows.
